■ While there were differences across states in the total quantum of foodgrains lifted, most states and UTs had seen a significant increase in offtake since FY 2020-21. Similar to the national trend, allocations and offtake of foodgrains reached its peak in FY 2021-22, with 11 states seeing an increase of more than 70 per cent in foodgrains lifted as compared to FY 2019-20.
