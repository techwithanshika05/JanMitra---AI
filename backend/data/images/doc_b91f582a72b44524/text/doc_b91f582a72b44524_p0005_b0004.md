■ In December 2022, the GoI announced its decision to distribute foodgrains free-of-cost to all citizens eligible under NFSA for a period of one year, starting from 1 January 2023. This new integrated scheme, also called PMGKAY, will subsume the two current Food Subsidy schemes: a) Food Subsidy to FCI for NFSA (CPS), and b) Food Subsidy for DCP for the states, dealing with procurement, allocation, and delivery of free foodgrains to the states under NFSA.
