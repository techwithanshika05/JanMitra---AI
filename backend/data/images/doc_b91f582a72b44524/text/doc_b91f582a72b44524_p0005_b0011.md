■ Not all allocations, however, were lifted. Since FY 2017-18, although the percentage of foodgrain lifted has consistently been above 80 per cent, it has also been decreasing across the years. In FY 2021-22, while the total quantum offtake of foodgrains increased significantly, as a share of total allocations it was only 89 per cent. Similarly, for FY 2021-22 till November, 585 lakh tons of foodgrains had been lifted, which was 80 per cent of the total allocation.
