■ Looking at the phase-wise trends specifically for PMGKAY, there are some differences in the proportion of foodgrains lifted. The phases implemented during the peak of the COVID-19 pandemic, namely PMGKAY-I (April to June 2020) and PMGKAY-III (May-June 2021) saw the highest proportion (98 per cent) of grains lifted out of total allocations. On the other hand, it was marginally lower for other phases in FY 2020-21 and FY 2021-22.
