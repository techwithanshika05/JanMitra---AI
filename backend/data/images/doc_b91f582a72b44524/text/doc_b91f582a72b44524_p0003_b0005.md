■ Allocations for Food Subsidy are released by GoI to FCI. Release of funds by GoI has been falling since FY 2013-14, resulting in large arrears and increase in debt burden as the dues are carried over to the next financial year. To meet its debt obligation, FCI borrows funds through Cash Credit Limit (CCL), Ways and Means Advances (WMA), Short Term Loan (STL), National Small Saving Fund (NSSF) Loan, and bonds.
