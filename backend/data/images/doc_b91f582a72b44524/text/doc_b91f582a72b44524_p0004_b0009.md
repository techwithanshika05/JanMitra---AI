o **DCP:** Under the DCP, state governments undertake direct purchase of foodgrains, and are also responsible for storage and distribution under NFSA and other welfare schemes. Excess stocks procured by the states are returned to FCI in the Central Pool, and the cost incurred on these excess stocks are reimbursed by FCI to state governments as per GoI costs sheets. GoI assumes the responsibility for the entire expenditure incurred during the procurement operations as per the pre-approved costing.
