■ PMGKAY was in operation for a total of 28 months; after initially being implemented for a period of three months from April 2020 to June 2020 (PMGKAY-I), the scheme was later extended till November 2020 (PMGKAY-II). In FY 2021-22, the scheme was restarted for a two-month duration in May 2021 (PMGKAY-III) and continued for a five-month period till November 2021 (PMGKAY-IV). Its fifth phase ran from December 2021 to March 2022. In FY 2022-23, PMGKAY-VI was implemented for 6 months from April to September 2022, and a final seventh phase of the scheme was announced from October to December 2022.
