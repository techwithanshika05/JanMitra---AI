It is proposed to establish an Anti-Fraud Management Cell as an independent vertical in NHA, headed by an Executive Director, reporting to Chief Executive Officer (NHA). Some of the roles/responsibilities may have some overlap with presently planned functions of Medical audit, grievance and vigilance teams, however it is felt that an independent anti-fraud vertical is critical for focused efforts and results in this area. The overlapping roles/functions, will be reviewed and streamlined to ensure synergies, avoid duplication of effort, and, for greater efficiency.
