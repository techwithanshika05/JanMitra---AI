■ The ‘One Nation, One Ration Card’ (ONORC) scheme was launched in August 2019 to allow for nationwide portability of NFSA benefits. The scheme provides flexibility to migrant eligible citizens to lift foodgrains from any FPS of their choice in most parts of the country, by using their existing ration card with biometric authentication of identity on an electronic Point of Sale (ePoS) device. ONORC is currently being implemented in all 36 states and UTs, with Assam being the latest state to join in June 2022.
