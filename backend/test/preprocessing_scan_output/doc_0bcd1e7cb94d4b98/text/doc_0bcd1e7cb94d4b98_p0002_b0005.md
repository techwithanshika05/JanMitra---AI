■ Looking deeper into transactions conducted by households from Bihar under ONORC, Delhi accounted for 76 per cent transactions in FY 2021-22, which increased to 79 per cent (9,17,479) in FY 2022-23. For transactions in Haryana from households in Bihar, a similar rise was recorded from 6 per cent in FY 2021-22 to 10 per cent in FY 2022-23 till December 2022.
