■ In FY 2021-22, ONORC was accessed by 73 lakh eligible citizens. There was consistent growth in the number of people accessing ONORC till October 2021. However, November 2021 saw a dip with only 5.7 lakh eligible citizens accessing ONORC. Consequently, in November 2021, the total number of transactions excluding PMGKY was only 1.3 lakh. In FY 2021-22, the total number of transactions under ONORC (16.5 lakh) were only 0.08 per cent of the total e-transactions.
